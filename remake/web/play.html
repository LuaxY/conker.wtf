Conker's Bad Fur Day

    

    
Waking up the Tediz (loading the game)…

    Click to capture the mouse (Esc frees it and pauses until the next click).
      Keys: WASD or arrows to move (hold Left Shift to walk), Space to jump, R to reload, Q to draw or put away a weapon,
      left mouse to fire, C to crouch, right mouse to aim (the mouse then aims, or moves a scope's look; the wheel
      zooms a scope), G for a grenade (hold to cook it), M mutes the sound, H shows a text readout of the HUD, F8 toggles
      the free debug camera, Tab opens the team screen in the team modes (left / right: team, W / S: BALANCE).
      Gamepad: left stick (L3 walks), A jump, X reload, Y draw, B crouch, right trigger fire, left trigger aim, left
      bumper grenade, right stick strafe, Select teams.
